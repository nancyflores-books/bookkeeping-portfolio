# Project 2: Bank Reconciliation
> **Fictional data. Practice demonstration, not client work.**

**Scenario:** Match Harbor Lane Studio's March bank statement to its books. **Objective:** Explain every difference. **Tools:** Xero (practice file), Excel.

| Ref | Bank | Books | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMT-1001 | 1,100.00 in | 1,100.00 | Matched |
| BILL-201 | 300.00 out | 300.00 | Matched |
| BILL-204 | not yet cleared | 1,200.00 out | Unmatched (timing) |
| Deposit | 350.00 in | recorded 2 Apr | Unmatched (in transit) |

## Summary
Bank balance 24,850.00 - 1,200.00 + 350.00 = **24,000.00**, equal to the ledger balance.

## Workflow
Import statement, match line by line, investigate unmatched items, document corrections.

## Deliverables
Reconciliation report and discrepancy notes.

## Key findings
Both differences were timing only. No corrections needed; they should clear in April.
