# Nancy Flores | Bookkeeping Portfolio

**Live site:** https://nancyflores-books.github.io/bookkeeping-portfolio/

A portfolio website for Nancy Flores, a remote bookkeeper trained in QuickBooks Online, Xero and Xero Payroll. It is written for business owners looking for bookkeeping support and for technical reviewers.

> **Disclosure:** all six case studies are training or practicum projects completed on practice companies with simulated data, through Accounting Ace Xero training and Hey! It's Kajea's 30-Day Bookkeeping Challenge. They are not paid client work. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xero or Intuit.

## Sample deliverables (fictional data)

Four short examples (workflow, bank reconciliation, financial reporting, AR and AP) use fictional data from a made-up studio. They show the format of the work. They are not real work, client engagements or testimonials.

## What the site includes

- Services, a monthly bookkeeping workflow, and skills and tools explained by how they help a client
- Proof screenshots that open larger when clicked
- Software certifications and course certificates, and a separate Recognition section for the Outstanding Intern certificate from Accounting Ace
- A data protection section that separates current practice from training
- A discovery call request form (Google Forms), email and LinkedIn links
- A responsive layout with scroll animation

## Technology

HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript, with no frameworks or build step. Google Fonts (Cormorant Garamond and Jost). Google Forms. GitHub Pages.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `python -m http.server` in the folder and visit http://localhost:8000

## Publish with GitHub Pages

In the repository, go to **Settings**, then **Pages**. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, the **main** branch and **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
