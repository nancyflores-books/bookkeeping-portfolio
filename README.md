# Nancy Flores | Bookkeeping Portfolio

**Live site:** https://nancyflores-books.github.io/bookkeeping-portfolio/

A portfolio website for Nancy Flores, a remote bookkeeper working with QuickBooks Online, Xero and Xero Payroll. It is written for two audiences: business owners looking for bookkeeping support, and technical reviewers looking at how the site is built.

> **Please note:** every project on this site is training or practicum work on practice companies, using simulated or fictional data. None of it is paid client work. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xero or Intuit.

## About

Nancy is a bookkeeper certified in QuickBooks Online, Xero and Xero Payroll, with practice experience across U.S., UK and Australian bookkeeping workflows (GST/BAS, PAYG, superannuation, UK VAT). She is open to remote bookkeeping, payroll support, and catch-up and clean-up projects.

## What the site includes

- Services, with a clear label showing which are backed by a case study
- Three practice case studies, written as Context, Action and Result, each with a screenshot that opens larger when clicked
- A "More practice samples" area with four fictional examples (workflow, bank reconciliation, financial reporting, AR and AP)
- A monthly bookkeeping workflow in seven steps, and a list of what a client receives each month
- Skills and tools, explained by how each helps a client, and a skills table with honest levels
- Certifications and training, shown as certificate images that open larger when clicked
- A "How I Protect Your Data" section that separates what is practiced today from what was covered in training
- A discovery call request form (Google Forms) and email, LinkedIn and case study buttons
- A responsive layout, scroll animation that follows the scroll position, and a menu that works on phones

## Featured case studies (practice data)

1. **From Bank Statement to Draft BAS in Xero:** 12 of 12 bank lines reconciled to a $0.00 difference, with 2 coding errors corrected using reversing journals.
2. **From 3 Pay Runs to a Quarterly BAS:** 3 monthly pay runs with PAYG and superannuation, matched to the quarterly Activity Statement.
3. **Cleaning Up Q1 Books and Reports in 7 Hours:** 19 errors corrected, 6 adjusting entries posted and a $0.00 Balance Sheet difference in all three months.

## Run it locally

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser.
3. Or run `python -m http.server` in the folder and visit http://localhost:8000

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. In the repository, go to **Settings**, then **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select the **main** branch and the **/ (root)** folder, then click **Save**.
4. After 1 to 3 minutes, the site is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/`.
