Bank Reconciliation and Draft BAS in Xero

Accounting Ace Xero training assignment | practice company, simulated data

Context

A practice company needed its bank reconciled, coding errors corrected and a draft Activity Statement (BAS) prepared in Xero.

Actions

  • Reconciled the bank statement lines in Xero.

  • Reviewed the draft Activity Statement.

Results

12/12 bank statement lines reconciled to a $0.00 difference
$642 payable on the draft Activity Statement ($12,700 sales incl. GST)
