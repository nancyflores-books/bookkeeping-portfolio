About Me

Messy books, skipped reconciliations, and looming BAS or VAT deadlines can cost business owners time and peace of mind. I help keep financial records accurate, organized, and up to date.

I'm a Bookkeeping Virtual Assistant certified in QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Xero Payroll, with practicum experience across U.S., UK, and Australian bookkeeping workflows.

My most relevant background is over ten years of part-time work as a Financial Record Assistant for a Mutual Fund Cooperative serving approximately 105–115 members. Twice a month, I recorded member share contributions, reconciled collections against loans released and cash on hand, and prepared financial records for audit review. I also maintained loan records, calculated interest and penalties, prepared year-end loan summaries, and updated individual member share records.

Process

My Monthly Bookkeeping Workflow

My documented workflow and training approach, step by step.

01

Collect

Request and collect documents such as bank statements, receipts, invoices and reports.

02

Import & Review

Import transactions from bank accounts and payment processors, then review them.

03

Categorize

Categorize transactions and record missing entries.

04

Reconcile

Reconcile bank, credit card and clearing accounts.

05

Review AR & AP

Review Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable.

06

Investigate

Investigate discrepancies and follow up on missing or inconsistent information.

07

Adjust

Record appropriate month-end adjustments after reviewing supporting documents.

08

Report

Deliver financial reports and a summary.

This is how I work through practice projects and training. Actual procedures depend on client requirements, access permissions and the agreed scope of work.
