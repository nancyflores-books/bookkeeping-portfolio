My Monthly Bookkeeping Workflow

A simple process for accurate books and clearer business decisions.

1
📂

Collect

Gather all required financial documents.

  • Bank statements
  • Credit card statements
  • Invoices & receipts
  • Sales reports (Shopify/Amazon)
  • Bills
  • Other supporting documents
2
📥

Record

Import and record transactions in Xero or QuickBooks Online.

  • Use bank feeds or CSV imports
  • Record missing transactions
  • Ensure all income and expenses are captured
3
🏷️

Categorize

Assign appropriate accounts and tax treatment.

  • Categorize income and expenses
  • Apply VAT/GST (where applicable)
  • Review and reclassify misclassified transactions
4
🏦

Reconcile

Match your books with bank and payment processor statements.

  • Bank reconciliation
  • Credit card reconciliation
  • Shopify/Amazon payouts
  • Payment processor clearing accounts (Stripe/PayPal, etc.)
  • Investigate and resolve discrepancies
5
🔍

Review

Check for accuracy, completeness and unusual items.

  • Review uncategorized transactions
  • Check for duplicates
  • Review AR & AP aging
  • Investigate unusual transactions
  • Ensure compliance with company standards
6
⚙️

Adjust

Prepare and record month-end adjustments (if needed).

  • Accruals and prepayments
  • Reclassifications
  • Depreciation (where applicable)
  • Payroll adjustments
  • Ensure all accounts are properly balanced
7
📊

Report

Deliver accurate and updated financial reports and insights.

  • Profit & Loss
  • Balance Sheet
  • Cash Flow (if available)
  • AR & AP reports
  • Management summary (if needed)

Actual procedures depend on client requirements, access permissions and the agreed scope of work.
