# Project 5: Rental Property Bookkeeping
> **Fictional data. Practice demonstration, not client work.**

**Scenario:** A fictional 3-unit building (Units A, B, C). **Objective:** Track rent, expenses and occupancy. **Tools:** Xero tracking categories (practice), Excel.

| Unit | Monthly rent | Jan | Feb | Mar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1,500 | Paid | Paid | Paid |
| B | 1,400 | Paid | Paid | **Unpaid** |
| C | 1,600 | Paid | Paid | Paid |

| Month | Rent collected | Expenses | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | 4,500 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Feb | 4,500 | 1,700 | 2,800 |
| Mar | 3,100 | 1,300 | 1,800 |
| **Q1** | **12,100** | **4,500** | **7,600** |

Occupancy 100% (3 of 3). Collection rate 12,100 / 13,500 = 89.6%.

## Workflow, deliverables, findings
Record rent by unit, code expenses by property, compare to expected rent. Deliverables: rent roll and property-level summary. Lesson: tracking by unit exposes late rent quickly.
